# Webwise vs named competitors + service-menu plan

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Sources:** live fetch of competitor homepages/pricing + prior Webwise audit. No invented conversion rates.

Webwise must **not** try to beat these five as a cheaper WhatsApp API or a 5-minute chatbot. They sell **software**. Webwise should sell a **done-for-you customer acquisition system** for clinics, property, and other local operators — using those platforms as pipes when needed.

## 3. Positioning sentence (use in sales, not live site until confirmed)

> Platforms sell you WhatsApp and a bot. Webwise installs the system around it: the page patients/buyers land on, the approved replies, the lead record, the human handoff, and the follow-up — live in 14 days for one niche.

**Vs Hyperleap:** They win “I will paste my URL and try $40.” You win “I need clinic-safe / site-visit routing, a new site, reviews, and someone accountable.”  
**Vs Wati/AiSensy:** They win broadcasts, green tick, CTWA. You win **enquiry → booking request → reminder → review** as an operating system. You may **use** them as the API.  
**Vs Engati/Gupshup:** Do not bid. Refer or white-label only if an agency brings an enterprise logo.

**Product D — Partner**  
White-label: you build CAS; they own the client. Do not try to be a BSP.

**Not on the homepage menu:** SMM, branding, six named AI job titles, “AI consulting” as a service line.

## 6. What not to do

- Headline the site with the 12-line list. That loses to both Hyperleap (clearer product) and a local SEO shop (clearer agency).  
- Promise Instagram + Messenger **as live channels** unless a Meta API is actually connected (homepage claims it; Hyperleap is a Meta Tech Provider and still prices it).  
- Race Hyperleap on “5 minutes” or $40. Your FAQ is **14 days**. That is a feature if delivery is real.  
- Race AiSensy on ₹2,500 chatbots. You will lose on price and they will win on API.
