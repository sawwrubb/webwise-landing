The Gap

Your reception is busy. Patient enquiries do not wait.

A patient checks your Google listing, sees a service they need, and sends a WhatsApp message. Your team is with patients, on calls, or closing the clinic. That enquiry waits.

The problem is not that your receptionist is doing poor work. The problem is that too many important handoffs rely on one person being free at exactly the right minute.
