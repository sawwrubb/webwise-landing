The Gap

Property leads go cold before your team gets a fair chance to speak to them.

A buyer sees your listing on 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing, Instagram or your website and enquires about a project, budget range or locality. Then the lead lands in a queue.

Your agents are on site visits, with buyers, managing documents or travelling. By the time someone replies, the buyer has checked three more listings and another broker has already offered a viewing.

What Webwise fixes

Webwise protects the handoff between interest and a booked site visit: faster first response, cleaner buyer context, locality-aware routing, and follow-up that does not depend on an agent remembering every open conversation.

Confirmations, location pins, reminders, rescheduling, agent contact, post-visit feedback and next-step follow-up.
